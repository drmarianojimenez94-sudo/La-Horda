## Security change

A replacement socket reusing a previous clientId could retain its previous exclusive champion without fresh entitlement. The relay now revalidates the preserved champion against the reconnecting verified account. This complements existing create/join/update grant checks and server-derived public founder identity.

## Practical limits

The match remains host-authoritative. The relay verifies sender slots and grants, but does not independently simulate competitive outcomes; a modified host can cheat. These checks establish functioning casual competition, not trusted ranked integrity. Browser contexts on one machine validate networking behavior and responsive layout; they are not separate physical iPhones, real mobile latency, human enjoyment, or a universal champion matchup balance study. Neither screenshots nor passing tests justify claiming a measured 10/10.
